What it is

An instrument, not a consultancy

A PCCP is a document. Today, deciding whether a change stayed inside it is a judgement a human makes by reading prose and remembering what was filed. ENVOLOK turns that document into something a machine can check, and turns the decision into an artifact.

Compile

Your authorized PCCP becomes a control contract: permitted modification types, metric boundaries, allowed evidence sources, required validation tests, approved method versions, approval roles.

Ingest

Point it at the evidence folder your quality system already produces. Dataset, candidate, validation, authorization, release, deployed artifact.

Decide

Nineteen named checks. A hash chain running from dataset through candidate, validation, authorization, release and deployed artifact.

Sign

An Ed25519 receipt naming every check and its result. Your reviewer, your auditor, or FDA can verify it independently.

The problem that exists right now

The plan is enforceable. Nothing enforces it.

Section 515C of the FD&C Act lets you ship pre-authorized changes without a new submission. It also makes the boundary binding. FDA states the consequence of crossing it in plain language:

Deviations from the authorized PCCP reviewed in the marketing submission would generally cause the device to be adulterated and misbranded under sections 501(f)(1) and 502(o) of the FD&C Act … where appropriate, FDA may take legal or regulatory action against violations of prohibited acts, including, without limitation, seizure or injunction. FDA, Predetermined Change Control Plans for Medical Devices, draft guidance, 22 August 2024

The boundary lives in prose

The Description of Modifications is a document. Whether a change is inside it is decided in a meeting, from memory, under schedule pressure.

Nothing blocks at deploy time

A deviation is normally discovered in an audit, a complaint investigation, or an inspection — after the change has shipped to patients.

Zero is not unknown

Change control systems record what was checked. Few of them distinguish checked and passed from never measured.

The record is a narrative

What exists after the fact is a memo describing a judgement, not a signed artifact naming what was verified and by what rule.

What ENVOLOK solves

The gate runs before the change ships

BLOCK is enforced, not discovered

An out-of-scope modification returns BLOCK and names the failing check. It does not become a finding six months later.

UNKNOWN is a third verdict

An unresolved unknown does not pass and does not fail. It returns UNKNOWN, because a thing nobody measured is not a thing that passed.

The receipt is the record

Ed25519, naming all nineteen checks and their results. Flip the decision, flip one check, swap a hash or backdate the timestamp and verification fails.

Evidence integrity, not just scope

Edit a manifest without re-signing it and ENVOLOK refuses to grade the change at all. Sloppy evidence is caught before the boundary is even considered.

Validation

Tested against FDA's own worked examples

FDA's PCCP guidance publishes eight worked examples in Section VIII. For each device the agency names the modifications that generally may be included in a PCCP and the ones that are not. That is 47 labelled cases, published by the regulator. ENVOLOK was run against every one.

Software, in vitro diagnostic, implantable and surgical hardware. The evidence chain is fully re-signed for every case, so the PCCP boundary is the only thing that can reject.

What this validation does not claim. FDA's out-of-scope list for Example 8, the implantable pulse generator, is beyond the retrievable text of the published guidance, so Example 8 was run in-scope only — three of a possible six or more cases. The harness prints that on its own output rather than quietly reporting 47 of 47.

These are FDA's illustrative examples, not a device maker's filed PCCP. The contents of authorized PCCPs are generally not in the public record: a clearance letter confirms one exists, but the 510(k) summary is written by the sponsor and is not required to describe it. Validation against a real filed PCCP requires a device maker who has one. That is the next rung, and it is the reason to talk.
Why now

The obligation is new, growing, and permanent

2022 — the law

FDORA added section 515C to the FD&C Act, creating PCCPs as a statutory pathway for pre-authorized device modifications.

2024–2025 — the guidance

FDA published draft guidance for all device types and final guidance for AI-enabled device software functions, with worked examples and a decision flowchart.

Now — the volume

Every authorized PCCP is a standing change-control obligation that lasts the life of the device. Each one adds recurring work that does not scale with headcount.

Next — the scope

Cybersecurity impact is expected inside the PCCP for each modification class. The number of things to verify per change is going up, not down.
